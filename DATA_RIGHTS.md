# Data use and limits

Source: [EPA ECHO bulk downloads](https://echo.epa.gov/tools/data-downloads), [ICIS-Air dictionary](https://echo.epa.gov/tools/data-downloads/icis-air-download-summary) and [About the Data](https://echo.epa.gov/resources/echo-data/about-the-data).

EPA explicitly provides the bulk files for developer and analyst use. Public study data are minimal factual codes/dates with complete SHA256 keys and attribution. No blanket copyright or CC0 claim is made over submitted state/local content. Full downloads, identity mappings and unused source material stay private. Code licensing does not grant rights over excluded source material. ECHO's information is informational and may be incomplete, late or corrected; its national records do not establish a facility's legal status. Source guidance warns against robotic queries; this study uses one published bulk download.

No owner/contact/address/location fields, credentials, private records or formal-publication manuscripts are public. Hashes do not guarantee anonymity. A recorded failed stack test is not an allegation of current noncompliance.
